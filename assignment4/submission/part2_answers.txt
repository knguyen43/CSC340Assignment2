BASIC SORTING ALGORITHM: SELECTION SORT

The Selection Sort algorithm sorts an array by repeatedly finding the minimum element from the unsorted part and putting it at the beginning.

Initial Array: [64, 25, 12, 22, 11, 90, 33, 7]

Iteration 1:
- Find minimum in positions 0-7: 7 at position 7
- Swap 64 and 7
Array after iteration: [7, 25, 12, 22, 11, 90, 33, 64]

Iteration 2:
- Find minimum in positions 1-7: 11 at position 4
- Swap 25 and 11
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 90, 33, 64]

Iteration 3:
- Find minimum in positions 2-7: 12 at position 2
- Already in correct position, no swap needed
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 90, 33, 64]

Iteration 4:
- Find minimum in positions 3-7: 22 at position 3
- Already in correct position, no swap needed
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 90, 33, 64]

Iteration 5:
- Find minimum in positions 4-7: 25 at position 4
- Already in correct position, no swap needed
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 90, 33, 64]

Iteration 6:
- Find minimum in positions 5-7: 33 at position 6
- Swap 90 and 33
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 33, 90, 64]

Iteration 7:
- Find minimum in positions 6-7: 64 at position 7
- Swap 90 and 64
Array after iteration: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 33, 64, 90]

Final Sorted Array: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 33, 64, 90]

FAST SORTING ALGORITHM: MERGE SORT

The Merge Sort algorithm is a divide and conquer algorithm that divides the input array into two halves, recursively sorts them, and then merges the sorted halves.

Initial Array: [64, 25, 12, 22, 11, 90, 33, 7]

Divide Step 1:
- Divide into: [64, 25, 12, 22] and [11, 90, 33, 7]

Divide Step 2:
- Divide [64, 25, 12, 22] into: [64, 25] and [12, 22]
- Divide [11, 90, 33, 7] into: [11, 90] and [33, 7]

Divide Step 3:
- Divide [64, 25] into: [64] and [25]
- Divide [12, 22] into: [12] and [22]
- Divide [11, 90] into: [11] and [90]
- Divide [33, 7] into: [33] and [7]

Merge Step 1:
- Merge [64] and [25] into [25, 64]
- Merge [12] and [22] into [12, 22]
- Merge [11] and [90] into [11, 90]
- Merge [33] and [7] into [7, 33]

Merge Step 2:
- Merge [25, 64] and [12, 22] into [12, 22, 25, 64]
- Merge [11, 90] and [7, 33] into [7, 11, 33, 90]

Merge Step 3:
- Merge [12, 22, 25, 64] and [7, 11, 33, 90] into [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 33, 64, 90]

Final Sorted Array: [7, 11, 12, 22, 25, 33, 64, 90]
